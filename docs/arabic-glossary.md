# Arabic style guide and glossary

The rules every Arabic string in Lunia follows. The decisions behind them are in `docs/PRD-arabic-gulf.md`.

## 1. Register by surface

| Surface | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Website, booking, customer account, gift cards, reviews, WhatsApp/SMS/email | **Saudi "white" Gulf dialect**: warm, short, natural. Light on heavy Najdi slang, so it reads well across the Gulf. | «احجزي موعدك الحين» · «صار خطأ، حاولي مرة ثانية» |
| Consent forms, health questionnaire, signed documents, invoices, legal/tax text | **Clear formal Arabic, soft tone**. No slang. Short sentences. | «يُرجى قراءة النموذج بعناية» · «فاتورة ضريبية مبسطة» |
| Staff admin | **Plain Gulf workplace Arabic**: the short labels Saudi staff use. Formal grammar, everyday words. | «حفظ» · «إلغاء» · «مواعيد اليوم» · «تم الحفظ» |

## 2. Who we address

- **Customers:** always feminine (احجزي، تواصلي، اختاري، بشرتك، لكِ only where it's needed to avoid ambiguity). This applies everywhere customers read, including WhatsApp, SMS and email.
- **Staff (admin):** neutral imperative or verbal noun for buttons (حفظ، إضافة، تعديل). Sentences address staff neutrally («اختر» is avoided; use «اختيار …» or rephrase).
- **The clinic speaks as "we"** (نرسل، بنتواصل معك، نسعد بزيارتك).

## 5. Spelling and format

- Write standard spelling even in dialect: نتائج (not نتايج), دقائق (not دقايق), شيء/شي (use «شي» only in casual marketing copy).
- «جاري» without tanween, consistently.
- Western digits (0–9) in forms, codes, prices and phone numbers. Arabic-Indic digits only in running marketing text, where they already appear.
- Brand and product names stay in Latin script: Lunia → «لونيا» in sentences, but product names (PCA SKIN, LED) stay as is.
- Punctuation: Arabic comma «،» and question mark «؟». Ellipsis «…».
